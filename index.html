Founder Story

Meet Shiv Narayan Gupta

Gupta Ji Bhujiya Wala is a beloved name in Basak Bagan, Patipukur. Since 1990, Shiv Narayan Gupta has built a reputation for sincerity, quality, and dependable service that families trust every day.

What started as a neighborhood shop has grown into a reliable local destination for bhujia, namkeen, groceries, household supplies, and daily essentials. The shop continues to win loyalty through honest service and a personal connection with every customer.
